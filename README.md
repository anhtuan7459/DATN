# DATN

## Huong dan dung Zotero de luu tru va quan ly bai bao

Zotero la cong cu giup luu tru, sap xep, trich dan va dong bo tai lieu tham khao (journal paper, conference paper, book, report...).

### 1. Cai dat ban dau

1. Cai Zotero Desktop: https://www.zotero.org/
2. Cai Zotero Connector tren trinh duyet (Chrome/Edge/Firefox) de luu bai bao nhanh.
3. Dang nhap tai khoan Zotero de su dung tinh nang dong bo.

### 2. Luu bai bao vao Zotero

1. Mo trang bai bao (IEEE, ACM, Springer, ScienceDirect, Google Scholar...).
2. Bam icon Zotero Connector tren trinh duyet.
3. Chon Collection (thu muc) muon luu.
4. Kiem tra metadata sau khi luu: title, author, year, DOI, journal.

Luu y:
- Neu trang cho phep, Zotero co the tu dong kem file PDF.
- Neu chua co PDF, co the keo tha file PDF vao item trong Zotero de dinh kem thu cong.

### 3. Quan ly bai bao trong Zotero

1. Tao Collection theo chu de (VD: Related Work, Method, Dataset, Evaluation).
2. Gan Tag de loc nhanh (VD: survey, benchmark, SOTA).
3. Dung Saved Search de tu dong nhom bai theo dieu kien (nam, tu khoa, tag...).
4. Them Note cho moi bai: tom tat, diem manh, han che, y tuong ap dung.
5. Dung plugin (neu can):
	- Better BibTeX: xuat file .bib on dinh cho LaTeX.
	- ZotFile: ho tro quan ly file PDF va doi ten file.

### 4. Trich dan va tao danh muc tai lieu tham khao

1. Cai Zotero Word/LibreOffice plugin (thuong co san trong Zotero).
2. Trong Word: Add/Edit Citation de chen trich dan.
3. Chon style trich dan (IEEE, APA, ACM...).
4. Bam Add/Edit Bibliography de tao danh muc tai lieu tu dong.

### 5. Danh sach bai bao hien co trong Zotero

Danh sach duoc tong hop tu collection ban dang mo:

1. A CNN-Based Method for Enhancing Boring Vibration with Time-Domain Convolution-Augmented Transformer (Zhang et al.)
2. A systems model and simulation of the vibratory bowl feeder (Maul and Brian Thomas)
3. CNN-Based Automatic Tablet Classification Using a Vibration-Controlled Bowl feeder with Spiral Torque Optimization (Yoon et al.)
4. Detecting industrial motor faults with current signatures (Krishnan and Vengadasalam)
5. Non-Intrusive Load Monitoring by Voltage-Current Trajectory Enabled Transfer Learning (Liu et al.)
6. Vision-based Vibrator Calibration (Malecki and Selma)


## Checklist de quan ly tai lieu hieu qua

- Tao cau truc Collection ro rang ngay tu dau.
- Chuan hoa tag (viet thuong, khong trung nghia).
- Moi bai bao deu co note tom tat 3-5 dong.
- Kiem tra DOI/nam/tac gia de tranh loi trich dan.
- Dinh ky export file .bib du phong.
